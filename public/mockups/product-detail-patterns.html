1 · Slide-in spec drawer

Click a card — a drawer slides from the right with tabbed specs (Overview / Materials / Impact) and a stack of outbound links, similar to a product-detail sidebar.

2 · Flip ticket

Click a card — it flips 180° like a lab ticket, revealing specs and links on the reverse face. Click again (or the "back to front" hint) to flip back.

3 · Inline accordion

Click a card — it expands in place within the grid (numbered-list style), pushing neighbors aside to reveal specs and links without leaving the page.

4 · Bottom sheet

Click a card — a full-width sheet rises from the bottom with a large hero, pill-style spec chips, and primary/secondary link buttons.

5 · Anchored popover

Click a card — a compact popover opens right next to it (catalog-browse style), showing a condensed spec table and link chips without leaving the grid.
