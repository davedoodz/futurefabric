# Material Brand Links Research Findings

## Overview
Official URLs for 6 material brand and designer collaboration products to power frosted product bar outbound links.

---

## Findings by Product Code

### MFB-02: MycoWorks Reishi Garment
- **Official URL**: https://www.mycoworks.com/blog/reishi-mycelium-material
- **Domain**: mycoworks.com
- **Rationale**: Official MycoWorks material product page for Reishi™, their flagship mycelium leather; brand homepage fallback: https://www.mycoworks.com

### PBC-04: Salvatore Ferragamo Orange Fiber Collection
- **Official URL**: https://sustainability.ferragamo.com/en/products-materials/orange-fiber-capsule-collection
- **Domain**: ferragamo.com (sustainability portal)
- **Rationale**: Dedicated official Salvatore Ferragamo page for the Orange Fiber Capsule Collection, presented April 22, 2017

### MFB-03: Stella McCartney × Bolt Threads Mylo Bustier & Trousers
- **Official URL**: https://www.stellamccartney.com
- **Domain**: stellamccartney.com
- **Rationale**: Official brand homepage; specific one-off pieces from March 2021 not retained as archived product pages; Bolt Threads collaboration: https://boltthreads.com

### MFB-04: Mogu Coprinus Garment
- **Official URL**: https://mogu.bio
- **Domain**: mogu.bio
- **Rationale**: Official Mogu brand homepage (Italy-based mycelium materials company); Mogu specializes in interior materials and textiles; Coprinus reference appears in designer collaborations using mycelium fiber (e.g., Nick Fouquet limited-run hats)

### PBC-05: Osman Yousefzada × Lenzing Tencel Luxe Garment
- **Official URL**: https://www.lenzing.com/newsroom/news-events/tenceltm-luxe-on-track-to-achieve-strong-growth-in-2022-as-eco-couture-movement-booms/
- **Domain**: lenzing.com
- **Rationale**: Official Lenzing newsroom article highlighting TENCEL™ Luxe growth, featuring Osman Yousefzada SS22 collection debut at London Fashion Week, September 20, 2021

### PRO-02: Yuima Nakazato × Spiber Brewed Protein Couture
- **Official URL**: https://spiber.inc/en/yuimanakazato/cosmos/
- **Domain**: spiber.inc
- **Rationale**: Official Spiber page showcasing Yuima Nakazato's Cosmos couture collection using Brewed Protein™ materials; additional collections at https://spiber.inc/en/news/

---

## Summary
- **Coverage**: 6 of 6 target products have official URLs identified
- **Priority**: All links are direct official brand, material-maker, or official announcement pages
- **Fallback approach**: Brand homepages provided for one-off pieces not retained as public product pages
