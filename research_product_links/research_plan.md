# Official product-link research

Question: identify the strongest official brand or material-maker URL for each FutureFabric product so the frosted product bar has one outbound link.

Subtopics:
- Brand product and collection pages for fashion brands.
- Material-maker pages for Piñatex, VEGEA, Reishi, Mylo, Circ, Tencel Luxe, and Brewed Protein.
- Official sustainability or product pages for Allbirds, The North Face, Xtep, and E. Marinella.

Synthesis: prefer a current official product page, then an official collection/material page, then the official brand homepage. Avoid retailer, editorial, and marketplace URLs.
